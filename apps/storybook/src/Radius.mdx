# Elevation, glass and light

- **Card:** `card` gradient + `shadow-card`, **no border at rest**, radius 26.
- **Spotlight (default hover):** on pointer move the card gets a 1px edge that is lit only near the cursor (a radial gradient masked to a 1px ring) plus a soft inner glow; it fades in over 350ms on hover **and** `:focus-within`. `useSpotlight` + CSS.
- **Panel:** nested surface (`panel` fill + inset `line`, radius 20).
- **Glass (floating only):** composer, suggestion chips, sticky bars, toasts, menus and dialogs – `blur(24px) saturate(140%)`, opaque enough that content beneath never shows through legibly.
- **AppBackground:** `bg` + soft top-left `ambient-glow` + one blurred diagonal `ambient-beam` drifting very slowly (static under reduced motion).
- `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` → cards become solid `surface`, glass becomes solid `raised`.
